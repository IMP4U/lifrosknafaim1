# Hida Wagyu Price Research

Research notes on Hida Wagyu pricing, comparison with other Japanese Wagyu
brands, and **butcher-counter market prices** in Takayama, Gifu Prefecture.
Compiled May 2026.

> Scope clarification: prices below are **raw meat at butcher shops / market
> counters** for take-home — not restaurant menu prices.

## 1. Can ¥10,000 buy 1 kg of A5 Hida Wagyu at the butcher?

**It depends on the cut.** At the butcher counter, ¥10,000 buys very
different amounts depending on whether you want a steak block or
yakiniku/sukiyaki slices.

| What you buy with ¥10,000 (A5 Hida)        | Approx. quantity |
|--------------------------------------------|------------------|
| Premium A5 sirloin / ribeye **steak block** | ~240 g           |
| A5 **yakiniku / sukiyaki slices** (chuck roll, round, ribeye offcuts, zabuton) | ~700 g – 1 kg |
| Thin-slice trim / offcuts (Maruaki)         | ~4 kg            |

So **¥10,000 ≈ 1 kg of A5 Hida is realistic for yakiniku-grade sliced cuts**
(¥1,000–¥1,400 / 100 g range), but **not** for premium A5 sirloin or ribeye
steak blocks (~¥4,100 / 100 g).

## 2. Hida Wagyu vs. Kobe, Matsusaka, Omi

| Brand      | Prefecture | Heritage                                | Flavor profile                       | Relative price          |
|------------|------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Kobe       | Hyogo      | Most globally famous                    | Fine even marbling, slightly sweet fat | Very high              |
| Matsusaka  | Mie        | Strict standards, low output            | Soft, aromatic fat                   | Often higher than Kobe  |
| Omi        | Shiga      | Oldest brand (>400 yrs), shogun-era     | Lighter, beef-forward                | High                    |
| **Hida**   | Gifu       | Founded 1982 from bull "Yasufuku"; 2002 Wagyu Olympics PM Award | Fine marbling, melt-in-mouth | **Best value** of the top-tier brands |

The "Holy Trinity" of Japanese Wagyu is **Kobe, Matsusaka, Omi**. Hida is a
younger but highly decorated brand competing on quality at a lower price
point. All four use the same JMGA A1–A5 grading system.

## 3. Hida Wagyu butcher / market prices in Takayama

**Maruaki (丸明)**, **Tohta (藤太)**, **Takumiya (匠家)**, **Tengu Sohonten
(天狗総本店)**, and **Hidatakayama-meat**. Prices in JPY, tax included.

### Lower grades and trim

| Item                                                 | Per 100 g | Per kg        |
|------------------------------------------------------|-----------|---------------|
| Thin-slice trim / offcuts (Maruaki butcher counter)  | ~¥250     | ~¥2,500       |
| A3–A4 yakiniku / stew cuts                            | ¥600–900  | ¥6,000–9,000  |
| A4 sirloin steak block                                | ¥2,500–3,500 | ¥25,000–35,000 |

### Reference benchmark

Generic A5 Japanese Wagyu ribeye (non-branded retail benchmark, 2025):
~¥25,900 / kg. Branded A5 Hida sirloin sits roughly 1.5× that mark; yakiniku
slices sit well below it.

### Where to save vs. splurge

- **Cheapest take-home**: Maruaki butcher counter (thin slices ~¥250/100g),
  good for hot-pot at the hotel.
- **Best value A5**: yakiniku and sukiyaki slices at ¥1,000–1,600 / 100 g —
  same beef as the steak blocks, but pre-sliced for grilling/hot-pot.
- **Special occasion**: A5 sirloin/ribeye steak blocks at ~¥4,000+/100 g, or
  filet at ¥5,000+/100 g.
- **Outside the tourist zone**: the Hida Beef Wholesale Processing Center
  prices are typically lower than old-town shops.
