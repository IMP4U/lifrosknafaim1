# Hida Wagyu Price Research

Research notes on Hida Wagyu pricing, comparison with other Japanese Wagyu brands, and market prices in Takayama, Gifu Prefecture. Compiled May 2026.

## 1. Can ¥10,000 buy 1 kg of premium A5 Hida Wagyu?

**No.** The "under ¥10,000" figure quoted by Takayama travel guides refers to a **restaurant meal per person**, not 1 kg of raw meat.

Reference prices for premium A5 Hida (raw, retail):

- A5 Hida sirloin: ~¥7,800 / 100 g (≈ ¥78,000 / kg)
- A5 Hida ribeye: ~¥7,500 / 100 g (≈ ¥75,000 / kg)
- General A5 Japanese Wagyu ribeye benchmark (2025): ~¥25,900 / kg (lower-grade / non-branded)

What ¥10,000 will actually buy:

- ~125–135 g of premium A5 Hida sirloin/ribeye at a butcher, OR
- A complete A5 Hida restaurant lunch/dinner for one person (steak + rice + sides)

## 2. Hida Wagyu vs. Kobe, Matsusaka, Omi

| Brand      | Prefecture | Heritage                        | Flavor Profile                     | Relative Price        |
|------------|------------|---------------------------------|------------------------------------|-----------------------|
| Kobe       | Hyogo      | Most globally famous            | Fine even marbling, slightly sweet fat | Very high             |
| Matsusaka  | Mie        | Strict standards, low output    | Soft, aromatic fat                 | Often higher than Kobe |
| Omi        | Shiga      | Oldest brand (>400 years), shogun-era | Lighter, beef-forward         | High                  |
| **Hida**   | Gifu       | Founded 1982 from bull "Yasufuku"; 2002 Wagyu Olympics Prime Minister's Award | Fine marbling, melt-in-mouth | **Best value** of the top-tier brands |

The "Holy Trinity" of Japanese Wagyu is **Kobe, Matsusaka, Omi**. Hida is a younger but highly decorated brand that competes on quality at a lower price point. All four are graded on the same JMGA A1–A5 scale.

## 3. Hida Wagyu prices in the Takayama market

Typical retail prices at Takayama butchers (Maruaki, Matsuki-ushi, Hidatakayama-meat, etc.) and street stalls.

### Raw meat by grade and cut

| Grade / Cut                              | Per 100 g          | Per kg                |
|------------------------------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Thin-slice trim (sukiyaki/shabu offcuts) | ¥250–600           | ¥2,500–6,000          |
| A3 Hida — everyday cuts (stew/yakiniku)  | ¥800–1,500         | ¥8,000–15,000         |
| A4 Hida — sirloin/ribeye                 | ¥3,000–5,000       | ¥30,000–50,000        |
| **A5 Hida ribeye**                       | **~¥7,500**        | **~¥75,000**          |
| **A5 Hida sirloin**                      | **~¥7,800**        | **~¥78,000**          |
| A5 Hida tenderloin/filet                 | ¥9,000–12,000      | ¥90,000–120,000       |

### Street food and prepared dishes

| Item                                   | Price            |
|----------------------------------------|------------------|
| Hida beef skewer / nigiri / steam bun  | ¥300–800 each    |
| Restaurant lunch set                   | ¥2,000–3,000     |
| Restaurant dinner / teppanyaki course  | ¥6,000–10,000+   |

### Where to buy more cheaply

- **Hida Beef Wholesale Processing Center** — outside the tourist old town, lower fixed costs passed to customers.
- Direct-sales counters at butcher-restaurants (Maruaki sells thin slices from ~¥250/100 g).
